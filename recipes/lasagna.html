Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian pasta dish made of stacked layers of flat pasta sheets with fillings like ragù (meat sauce), béchamel or ricotta cheese, and mozzarella, topped with Parmesan and baked until golden and bubbly.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Sausage and ground beef
  • Crushed tomatoes and tomato paste
  • Sugar
  • Cheese
  • Egg

Steps

  1. Boil the pasta
  2. Prepare the lasagna ingredients
  3. Make the layers
  4. Rest before slicing

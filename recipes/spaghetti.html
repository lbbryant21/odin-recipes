Lasagna

Description

Spaghetti is a long, thin, cylindrical pasta from Italy, named from the Italian word for "lengths of cord". It's typically made from durum wheat semolina and water, sometimes with eggs, and is famously served with tomato sauce, but also pairs well with oil-based sauces, meat, or fish.

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti
  • Ground meat
  • Tomatoes and tomato paste
  • Onion, garlic, and spices

Steps

  1. Make quick meat sauce
  2. Cook spaghetti in salted water
  3. Cook to al dente
  4. Dont rinse spaghetti
